Position

The market isn't ignoring your company. It was never handed a reason to care.

Most small and mid cap businesses don't have a performance problem. They have a translation problem: a genuinely good company that the right investors have simply never heard articulated properly.

Washuvi was built by people who spent decades on the other side of that table, in institutional equity research, investor relations and financial journalism. Roughly fifty years between us, spent deciding which companies were worth backing, and why.

Equity research Investor relations Financial media IPO readiness

The problem

A good company the investors who move valuations have never heard explained properly.

What we do

Make your company impossible to overlook for mutual funds, family offices, PE desks and serious HNIs, and keep them engaged through every phase of the cycle.

The end state

A loyal, long term shareholder base, not a one quarter spike. We also handhold unlisted companies all the way to listing.

What conviction buys you

What a well run investor programme actually gets you.

  • Capital, before you need it

    The right investors arrive ahead of your raise, so growth is never held up by a funding round that came too late.

  • A valuation that holds

    A live, credible investor relations programme helps the market price your business on what it actually is, not on silence.

  • Resilience under fire

    A managed investor base and honest, proactive perception management protect value exactly when the business is tested.

  • Owners, not tourists

    Informed, engaged investors become long term holders. They dampen volatility and stand with you across the cycle.

  • Institutional trust

    Consistent, transparent communication earns the confidence of funds and family offices. It is the slow asset that compounds.

The whole strategy, in one line

We turn down more mandates than we accept. Attention is the only thing we sell, and it doesn't divide well.

Every investor relations firm wants more logos. We want fewer, because a capped roster is the only way senior partners stay on every account.

Approach

How a mandate runs, from first look to compounding.

  1. 1

    We research you first

    We don't pitch companies we haven't already researched. By the time we call, we've read the filings, walked the numbers and formed a real view. If we don't believe it, we don't call.

    • Then a deep dive: model, financials, founder vision, competition
  2. 2

    We write the equity story

    A single, defensible growth narrative, not a deck of adjectives. Then we shortlist the exact investor categories that story is built to win.

    • Built from your filings up, never a reused template
  3. 3

    We get you in the room, and keep you there

    Proactive outreach, quarterly concalls, management meetings, plant visits and perception management. A wider investor base becomes durable loyalty.

    • Monthly sector letters keep investors engaged between results

FAQ

Questions founders ask us first.

Something not covered here? Email admin@washuvi.com and we will reply within one business day.

Who do you work with?

Listed SMEs, growing unlisted businesses and corporates in India's small and mid cap space that want their investor engagement to finally work.

How many companies do you take on at a time?

Eight to ten, at any time. Attention is what we sell, so we cap the roster and turn down more mandates than we accept.

Who will actually handle our account?

Senior partners only. Junior staff do not run accounts at Washuvi.

What services do you offer?

Three mandates: tailored investor relations, strategic advisory, and stock placement. See what we do for the detail of each.

Do you work with unlisted companies?

Yes. We handhold unlisted companies all the way to being ready to list, including IPO preparation and pre IPO placement.

Which investors can you reach?

Mutual funds, family offices, PE desks and serious HNIs.

What happens between results?

We stay on: monthly sector newsletters, management meetings, plant visits and ongoing perception management, so investors never go a quarter without hearing from you.

What if we go through a difficult quarter?

Perception management and crisis support are part of every investor relations mandate. We aim to be most present when it is hardest.

How does an engagement start?

We research your company before we approach you. Once mandated, we go deep into the business model, financials, your vision and the competitive landscape, then write the equity story.
