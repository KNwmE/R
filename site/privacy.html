Privacy policy

Draft for legal review. This page was written from the information on the current website and has not yet been checked by a lawyer.

This policy explains what Washuvi Financial Advisory LLP ("Washuvi", "we") does with information you share through www.washuvi.com.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry, we receive the details you type into the form: your name, email address, and, if you choose to give them, your phone number, company, the mandate you are interested in and your message.

This site does not use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies of its own.

How the form is delivered

Enquiries are passed to us by Web3Forms, a form delivery service, which forwards them to our email inbox. Web3Forms processes the submission only to deliver it.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, usually within one business day.
  • To evaluate whether we can take on a mandate for your company.

We do not sell your information or share it for marketing.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, email admin@washuvi.com.
